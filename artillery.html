BROARTILLERY

two tanks. one keyboard. destroy the terrain, and your brother along with it.

Controls

  • A / D — drive (limited fuel)
  • Mouse or W / S — aim
  • Hold click / SPACE — charge power, release to FIRE
  • 1–5 or Q / E — pick a weapon
  • R — restart  ·  H — help  ·  M — mute
  • Rebind anything you like in the ⚙ panel (F2) — both players get their own keys.

The rules of war

  • Turn-based. Blow up your bro before he blows up you.
  • Wind changes every turn. It will betray you.
  • You can blow yourself up. It's very funny.
  • Blast a hole under him — gravity does the rest.
  • After round 11: THE GOO RISES.
