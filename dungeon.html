BRO DUNGEON
P1 A/D/W/S move · F sword · G dash  ·  P2 ←/→/↑/↓ move · J sword · K dash  ·  clear the room, then walk through the open door

HOW TO DESCEND

Player 1

  • A W S D — move
  • F — swing the sword (the arc in front of you)
  • G — dash (short burst, brief invulnerability)

Player 2

  • ← ↑ ↓ → — move
  • J — swing the sword
  • K — dash

What is down there

  • Skeletons walk straight at you. Bats are fast and dopey.
  • Ghosts drift through the walls. Swing at the room, not at them.
  • The last room has something the size of the fridge. Bring swords.
  • Coins are the score. Hearts heal two points. Blue orbs shield you.

Rules

  • Co-op: two heroes, eight rooms, one dungeon.
  • Clear every monster and the door on the right unlocks.
  • You can walk back through the door you came in by.
  • Want different keys? Hit ⚙ (F2) and rebind both players.
