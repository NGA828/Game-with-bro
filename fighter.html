HERO CLASH
P1 W A S D move · F light · G heavy · H special · T super  ·  P2 ← ↑ → ↓ move · J light · K heavy · L special · I super

HOW TO CLASH

Team Letters (P1)

  • A D — walk
  • W — jump  ·  S — crouch
  • F light · G heavy
  • H special (needs meter) · T SUPER at 100%
  • Hold back to block. Crouching blocks low hits, standing blocks high ones.

Team Numbers (P2)

  • ← → — walk
  • ↑ — jump  ·  ↓ — crouch
  • J light · K heavy
  • L special · I super
  • Gamepads work: stick to steer, X light, Y heavy, B special, A super.

How a round works

  • Best of three. 1000 HP, 60 seconds on the clock.
  • Meter builds when you hit and when you get hit.
  • Low hits beat a standing hero. High hits beat a crouching one.
  • Time out? Whoever has more health wins the round.

On the select screen

  • W A S D + F — P1 picks a hero
  • ↑ ↓ ← → + J — P2 picks a hero
  • C — VS CPU on/off  ·  V — difficulty
  • F1 — this help  ·  Esc — back to the hub
  • Prefer other keys? Hit ⚙ (F2) and rebind Player One and Player Two.
