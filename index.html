HOW THE HUB WORKS

PICK A GAME

The pop-up that greets you is the whole menu. Pick a game and it starts right away — no lobby, no waiting, no patch notes.

SHARE THE KEYBOARD

Every game here is hot-seat: two bros, one screen, taking turns or fighting side by side. Gamepads work too if you've got them.

YOUR KEYS, YOUR RULES

The ⚙ panel in the top bar opens the settings. Give Player One and Player Two whatever keys you like, set the music, and it sticks.

ALONE?

HERO CLASH and TURBO BROS have a VS CPU mode, so you can practise your lines before your bro gets home.
