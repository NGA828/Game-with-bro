PIZZA RUSH
P1 A/D pick topping · W add · S bake & serve  ·  P2 ←/→ pick topping · ↑ add · ↓ bake & serve  ·  match the ticket, then serve before they walk out

HOW TO SLICE

Player 1

  • A D — pick a topping from the bins
  • W — put that topping on the dough (press again to take it off)
  • S — put it in the oven, then press again when it is ready

Player 2

  • ← → — pick a topping
  • ↑ — add or remove that topping
  • ↓ — bake, then serve

How a ticket works

  • The paper by the window shows the toppings they asked for.
  • Add exactly those, bake it, and take it out when the oven says READY.
  • The tip grows the faster you serve them. Consecutive good pizzas build a combo.

Rules

  • A 75 second shift. Most tips wins.
  • Wrong pizza: no tip, no combo, and they lose patience.
  • Walk out: no tip, no combo, and a new customer immediately.
  • Want different keys? Hit ⚙ (F2) and rebind both players.
