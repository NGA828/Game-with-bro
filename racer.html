HOW TO DRIVE

Team Orange (P1)

  • W accelerate · S brake / reverse
  • A D steer
  • SPACE handbrake (drift!)
  • LSHIFT nitro

Team Blue (P2)

  • ↑ accelerate · ↓ brake / reverse
  • ← → steer
  • / handbrake
  • RSHIFT nitro

Race rules

  • Three laps, four cars, one screen.
  • Split screen when two bros play. Press C on the track screen for a solo race against the CPUs.
  • Hit the glowing pads for free speed.
  • The grass is slow. The barrier is slower.

Gamepads

  • Left stick — steer
  • RT — accelerate · LT — brake
  • A — handbrake · RB — nitro
  • Plug in two pads and the bro you pass will feel it.
  • Keyboard keys can be changed any time — hit ⚙ (F2).
