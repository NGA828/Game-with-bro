DUCK INVADERS
P1 A/D move · W/S up-down · F shoot  ·  P2

HOW TO QUACK

Player 1

  • A D — move left / right
  • W S — move up / down
  • F — shoot (hold to keep firing)

Player 2

  • ← → — move left / right
  • ↑ ↓ — move up / down
  • J — shoot

Pickups

  • S spread shot for 12 seconds
  • 1 extra life
  • O bubble shield
  • B screen bomb

Rules

  • Co-op: both bros defend the same city.
  • Every fifth wave is a boss duck. He has a lot of HP.
  • Three lives each. Getting hit drops you back in with a shield.
  • Survive all 8 waves to win the war.
  • Want different keys? Hit ⚙ (F2) and rebind both players.
